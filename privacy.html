Privacy Policy — IronLog MA

Last updated: November 2026

1. Local Data Storage

Your workout sets, profile metrics (age, height, weight, goals), hydration, and meal preferences are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences.

2. AI Food Camera

When you choose to scan a meal photo, the image is sent over HTTPS through our Cloudflare Worker proxy to the Google Gemini API for nutritional estimation. Do not upload sensitive or personal photos. We use Google's free Gemini API tier. Under Google's terms for that tier, Google may use content sent to it to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Processing by Google Gemini API is subject to Google API Terms of Service.

3. Security, Rate Limiting & Licensing

Our proxy temporarily processes your IP address and an anonymous app-generated device ID in Cloudflare storage (Durable Objects) solely for abuse/rate limiting and single-device activation code binding. Rate-limit counters are deleted automatically every day; activation code bindings are kept until the code is reset.

4. Advertising

The free version displays ads via Google AdMob, which may collect device advertising identifiers in accordance with Google policies. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and you can change your choice in Settings > Privacy & disclaimer.

5. Nutrition & Health Disclaimer

Calorie, macro, 1RM, and Ramadan mode calculations are informational estimates only and do not constitute medical or dietary advice. Values for Moroccan foods and AI meal scans are approximate and can be wrong.

6. Contact & License Reset

For privacy requests or to reset an activation code to a new device, email ayoubjawhar20@gmail.com.
